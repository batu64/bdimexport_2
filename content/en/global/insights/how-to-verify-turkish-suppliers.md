# How to Verify Turkish Suppliers: 6-Point Due Diligence Checklist Before Wiring a Deposit

Online B2B directories and trade show lists frequently mix genuine ISO-certified plants with one-person brokerage offices that own zero machinery. Whether you are evaluating **clothing manufacturers in Turkey**, **packaging manufacturers in Turkey**, **private label cosmetics manufacturers**, or heavy machinery builders, verifying corporate and physical manufacturing status **before** wiring a 30% deposit eliminates counterparty fraud and hidden subcontracting.

Here is the exact 6-point verification protocol our on-ground Turkish and US due-diligence desk executes within 48 to 72 hours.

## 1. Cross-Check MERSİS, Tax Plate (Vergi Levhası) and Trade Registry Gazette

Every legitimate commercial company in Türkiye holds three verifiable statutory records:

- **MERSİS Number (16-digit Central Registry System ID) & Tax ID (10-digit Vergi Kimlik Numarası):** Confirms active tax office registration, legal company type (*Anonim Şirketi — A.Ş.* or *Limited Şirketi — Ltd. Şti.*), and official registered address.
- **Turkish Trade Registry Gazette (*Türkiye Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi*):** Reveals incorporation date, paid-in capital, authorized signatories (*İmza Sirküleri*), shareholding changes, and official NACE business activity codes.
- **Bank IBAN Beneficiary Match:** Never wire funds to a personal account or an unrelated third-party company name. The Turkish corporate USD/EUR IBAN beneficiary must match the exact legal entity named on your Proforma Invoice and Signatory Circular.

## 2. Request the Industrial Capacity Report (*Sanayi Kapasite Raporu*) — The Ultimate Factory Proof

Unlike trading companies, an actual manufacturer in Türkiye holds an official **Capacity Report (*Kapasite Raporu*)** issued and physically audited by the local Chamber of Industry (*Sanayi Odası*) and TOBB. This single document proves:
- Exact factory floor address and owned vs. leased machinery list
- Registered engineer and production worker headcount
- Annual theoretical production capacity by product group and raw material consumption

If a vendor claiming to own a packaging, cosmetics, apparel, or furniture plant cannot produce a valid *Kapasite Raporu*, they are operating as an intermediary or outsourcing production.

## 3. High-Demand Sectors Where Verification Matters Most (Packaging, Cosmetics, Apparel & Stone)

- **Packaging Manufacturers in Turkey:** Verify food-contact migration certificates (BRCGS Packaging / ISO 22000), flexo/offset/rotogravure press ownership, and cylinder/mold ownership clauses.
- **Private Label Cosmetics & Personal Care Manufacturers:** Verify Ministry of Health GMP (*ISO 22716 Good Manufacturing Practices*), Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR) / EU CPNP & UK SCPN dossier capability, and in-house microbiology lab testing.
- **Apparel, Activewear & Workwear ([Apparel Hub](/en/products/apparel)):** Verify whether cutting, sewing, washing, and printing are in-house or subcontracted, and validate OEKO-TEX® / GOTS scope certificate numbers directly in public issuer databases.
- **Natural Stone & Timber Infrastructure ([Marble](/en/products/marble) & [Wooden Utility Poles](/en/wooden-utility-poles)):** Verify quarry license or autoclave cylinder instrumentation records prior to container booking.
