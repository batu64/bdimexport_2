# Import Duty from Türkiye to the UK & EU: UK-Turkey FTA Origin Rules vs. A.TR Certificate Explained

British and European buyers researching **import duty from Turkey to UK** or **ATR certificate Turkey** frequently run into outdated pre-Brexit customs advice. Since the United Kingdom left the European Union Customs Union, **A.TR movement certificates are no longer valid for imports into Great Britain** — yet most Turkish industrial goods still enter the UK at **0% customs duty** under the **UK–Türkiye Free Trade Agreement**, provided the shipment carries a valid **Origin Declaration**.

## 1. UK–Türkiye Free Trade Agreement vs. EU–Türkiye Customs Union (A.TR)

Understanding the legal distinction between *free circulation* (EU) and *preferential origin* (UK) prevents unexpected customs tariff bills at Dover, Felixstowe, or London Gateway:

| Destination Market | Governing Trade Framework | Required Customs Document for 0% Industrial Tariff | Critical Rule / Exception |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **United Kingdom (Great Britain)** | UK–Türkiye Free Trade Agreement | **Origin Declaration** on the commercial invoice (or movement certificate where applicable) | Proves *preferential Turkish origin* (sufficient processing in Türkiye); A.TR is **not** accepted by HMRC |
| **European Union (27 Member States)** | EU–Türkiye Customs Union (Decision 1/95) | **A.TR Movement Certificate** | Proves *free circulation* in Türkiye; excludes agricultural goods and certain ECSC coal/steel items (which use EUR.1) |
| **Switzerland, Norway & EFTA** | EFTA–Türkiye Free Trade Agreement | **EUR.1 / EUR-MED** or Approved Exporter Origin Declaration | Preferential origin rules apply |

## 2. How Import Tax & Postponed VAT Work When Importing from Türkiye to the UK

When importing commercial goods such as [clothing and private-label apparel](/en/products/apparel), [furniture](/en/products/furniture), [kitchenware](/en/products/kitchenware), or [ceramic tiles](/en/products/ceramics) from Türkiye into the UK:

1. **UK Global Tariff (Customs Duty):** **0%** for qualifying Turkish-origin industrial goods accompanied by a compliant UK–Türkiye FTA Origin Declaration and supplier origin records. Without valid origin proof, standard UK Global Tariff MFN rates (typically 2%–12% depending on commodity code) apply on CIF value.
2. **UK Import VAT (20% Standard Rate):** VAT-registered UK companies can use **Postponed VAT Accounting (PVA)** on their CDS customs declaration ( requiring a **GB EORI** number), accounting for import VAT on their quarterly VAT return with **zero upfront cashflow outlay at the border**.
3. **Transit Time Advantage:** Door-to-door curtain-sider road freight from Istanbul, Izmir, Bursa, or Denizli reaches UK distribution centers in **6 to 9 days**, while short-sea container services arrive in **10 to 14 days**.

## 3. Pre-Dispatch Document & Inspection Checklist for UK Importers

Before releasing final balance payment to a Turkish manufacturer:
- Verify the 10-digit **UK Trade Tariff commodity code** on GOV.UK and confirm rule-of-origin compliance.
- Check UKCA / CE product marking, UK textile fiber labeling, or UK Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988 compliance if importing upholstered [hospitality or residential furniture](/en/products/hospitality-furniture).
