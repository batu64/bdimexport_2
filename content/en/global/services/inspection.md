# Factory Inspection in Turkey: Check the Goods Before You Pay

The cheapest moment to find a quality problem is before the goods leave the factory. After shipment, the same problem means disputes, delays and cost. Our inspection service sends an independent inspector to the factory, checks your order against your specification and reports the result with photographs and measurements.

## What you receive

- An inspection plan agreed with you before the visit
- A written report within 48 hours of the visit, with photographs, measurements and a clear pass or fail result
- Defects classified as critical, major or minor, with counts and examples
- Practical advice when the lot fails: rework, sorting or re-inspection
- A quotation within 24h of receiving your order details

The report belongs to you. It is written for the buyer, not for the factory.

## Types of inspection

| Type | When | What it answers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Factory audit | Before the first order | Can this factory really make my product, and can it do so consistently? |
| Pre-production check | Before the full run starts | Are materials, samples and specifications confirmed? |
| During production check | When 20 to 50 percent of the order is made | Are problems appearing early, while correction is still cheap? |
| Pre-shipment inspection | When goods are packed | Does the finished order match the approved sample and the contract? |
| Loading supervision | During container loading | Are the right goods, quantities and packing in the container? |

For large or high-risk orders we recommend combining a mid-production check with a final inspection.

## How an inspection works

1. **Order details.** You send the purchase order, specification, approved sample and any test requirements.
2. **Inspection plan.** We prepare a checklist: quantity, workmanship, measurements, function, labelling, packaging and any special tests.
3. **Sampling.** We use AQL sampling in line with the ISO 2859-1 approach. You agree the inspection level and acceptable quality limits in advance.
4. **Visit.** The inspector selects cartons at random, checks the goods and records results on site.
5. **Report.** You receive the report within 48 hours of the visit, with photographs and a recommendation.
6. **Follow-up.** If the lot fails, we advise on rework or sorting and re-inspect when the factory is ready.

## What we check

- **Quantity and packing:** carton count, assortment, marking and labels
- **Appearance and workmanship:** visible defects, finishing, colour match to the approved sample
- **Measurements and tolerances:** dimensions, weight and fit against your specification
- **Function and safety:** operation, simple on-site tests and visible safety points
- **Packaging:** carton strength, drop test where agreed, barcodes and shipping marks

Laboratory testing, such as material composition or chemical tests, is arranged separately with an accredited laboratory and listed as its own cost line.

## Independent by design

An inspection only helps if the inspector works for you. Our inspectors report to the buyer, do not take instructions from the factory on results, and record what they see. If something is unclear, the report says so instead of hiding it.

## How to read the report

Every report ends with a clear recommendation, so you can act the same day:

- **Pass:** the lot meets the agreed limits and can be released for shipment
- **Pass with comments:** the lot is acceptable, but we list points the factory should correct before the next order
- **Fail:** defects exceed the agreed limits; we advise on sorting, rework and re-inspection
- **Hold:** the inspector could not complete the check, for example because the goods were not fully packed

We also add photographs of the main defects and of the packed goods, so you can show the evidence to the factory without translation or interpretation.

## Preparing the factory for the visit

Inspections go faster when the factory is ready. We ask the factory to have the complete order packed and available, the approved sample on site, and the specification and packing list printed. If the goods are not ready on the agreed day, we tell you at once, and you decide whether to reschedule. This avoids paying for a visit that cannot give you a useful result.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why not trust the factory's own quality report?
The factory has an interest in shipping the goods. An independent report gives you evidence that you can use in a payment or claim discussion.

### What does AQL mean?
AQL means acceptable quality limit. It is the maximum number of defects allowed in a random sample before a lot fails. You agree the limits before the inspection.

### How early should I book an inspection?
Book as soon as the production schedule is known. A pre-shipment inspection should take place when the goods are fully produced and at least most of them are packed.

### What happens if the order fails?
We explain the failure, classify the defects and advise on rework or sorting. After the factory corrects the problem, we re-inspect.

### Can you inspect food or technical products?
Yes, with a plan adapted to the product. Specialised checks such as laboratory analysis or technical acceptance tests are agreed in advance.

### Can inspection be combined with shipping?
Yes. After a passed inspection we can coordinate [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics) and the [export customs documents](/en/services/customs).
