# Container Loading Supervision and On-Site Inspection (Türkiye and China)

Buying directly from a factory you found yourself reduces unit cost, yet the highest financial risk occurs at the moment of dispatch. Wrong carton counts, unsealed containers, damp packaging, or substituted production batches are only discovered weeks later when the shipment clears customs at your destination port.

Bdimexport deploys local field inspectors across **both Türkiye and China** to supervise production and container loading on your behalf — **even when you sourced the supplier independently and do not use our full sourcing service**.

## Why Independent Supervision Matters Before Paying the Balance

Most B2B factories require a 30% deposit and 70% balance against copy of Bill of Lading or prior to loading. Without a field representative physically standing at the loading bay:

1. **Carton Tally Verification:** Our inspector counts every carton entering the container and cross-checks barcode / SKU markings against your commercial packing list.
2. **Container Weatherproofing:** Holes in container roofs or damaged door gaskets cause condensation and rust on steel, machinery, textile, and paper packaging during sea transit.
3. **Live Photo and Seal Proof:** You receive real-time WhatsApp or email photos of the open container, mid-loading stack, lashing, closed doors, and the serial-numbered bolt seal before the truck leaves the factory gate.
