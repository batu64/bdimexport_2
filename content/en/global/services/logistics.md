# Freight Logistics from Turkey: From the Factory Gate to Your Warehouse

Good goods can still arrive late, damaged or with the wrong paperwork. Our logistics service plans the whole route from the factory gate in Turkey to your warehouse. You get route options, costs and a dated plan before you commit, and one team follows the shipment until delivery.

## What you receive

- Route and cost options within 24h of receiving the shipment details
- A recommended option with transit time, cost and risks explained in plain language
- Booking, pickup from the factory, export handling and main carriage
- Tracking updates at each key point, from pickup to delivery
- Cargo insurance options, quoted as a separate line
- A final delivery summary with all documents for your records

## Transport modes

| Mode | Strengths | Think about |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Road (trucks, CMR) | Flexible, direct to Europe and nearby regions, door-to-door | Border queues and driver regulations affect timing |
| Sea (FCL and LCL) | Lowest cost per unit for heavy or bulky goods, global reach | Longer transit, port handling, schedule changes |
| Air | Fastest option for urgent or high-value goods | Highest cost, size and weight limits |
| Rail and multimodal | Alternative corridors and mixed routes for some destinations | Not available for every origin and destination |

We recommend a mode based on your cargo, deadline and budget. We do not push the most expensive option, and we say so when a cheaper route meets your deadline.

## How a shipment works

1. **Shipment details (day 1).** You send cargo description, weight, volume, packing, pickup location, destination and requested delivery date. You receive route and cost options in 24h.
2. **Decision and booking.** You choose an option. We book the carrier and confirm pickup time with the factory.
3. **Pickup and export handling.** The goods are collected, checked against the packing list and prepared for export. The export declaration is handled with your [customs documents](/en/services/customs).
4. **Main carriage.** The shipment moves by road, sea, air or rail. We follow it and inform you about delays early.
5. **Import side.** We coordinate with your broker or ours for import clearance, according to the Incoterm you agreed.
6. **Delivery.** The goods reach your warehouse. You receive proof of delivery and the document set.

## Incoterms and who pays what

The Incoterm decides who organises and pays each step. EXW means you take responsibility at the factory gate. FOB means the seller loads the goods onto the vessel at the port. CIF and similar terms include main carriage and insurance in the price. The wrong Incoterm can hide costs or leave you without control, so we explain the options before you sign the sales contract. Our [sourcing service](/en/services/sourcing) includes comparing offers on the same Incoterm.

## Packing and loading

Poor packing causes more damage than long routes. We review how the goods are packed, palletised and secured, and we recommend improvements when needed. For container shipments we plan loading to use space well and to protect the cargo. After a passed [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection), we can supervise loading so that what was inspected is what leaves the factory.

## Special cargo

- **Food and temperature-sensitive goods:** reefer trucks and containers, with temperature requirements agreed in advance
- **Heavy or oversized machinery:** special equipment and route planning
- **Fragile goods:** reinforced packing and handling instructions
- **Dangerous goods:** only with the correct classification, packaging and documents; tell us early if your cargo is regulated

## Cost transparency

Our quotation lists freight, local charges, export handling, insurance and our service fee as separate lines. Charges that depend on events outside our control, such as waiting time or storage, are explained in advance, so you know when extra costs are possible.

## Tracking and communication

You should never have to chase for news. We send a short status update at each key point: pickup, export release, departure, arrival at the port or border, and delivery. If a delay appears, we tell you the cause, the expected new date and the options available. When the situation depends on a carrier or an authority, we say what we know, what we do not know yet, and when we will check again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How quickly do I get a freight quotation?
You receive route and cost options within 24 hours of sending complete shipment details. Incomplete details can delay the answer, so include weight, volume and destination.

### How long does transit take?
It depends on the destination, mode, border situation and season. The quotation shows the expected transit time for each option, and we update you if it changes.

### Can you ship small quantities?
Yes. Groupage (LCL) and parcel-type options exist for smaller shipments. They can cost more per unit than full loads, and we show you the comparison.

### Do you insure the cargo?
We offer cargo insurance as an option. The cover and the cost depend on the goods and the route, and it appears as a separate line in the quotation.

### Who handles customs?
Export clearance is handled with the export documents described on our [customs page](/en/services/customs). Import clearance is handled by your broker or by a partner broker, depending on the destination and the Incoterm.

### Can you combine shipments from several suppliers?
Yes, where the suppliers are located close together. Consolidation can reduce freight cost per unit, but it adds a handling step, so we explain the trade-off first.
