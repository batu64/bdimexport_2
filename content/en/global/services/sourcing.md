# Sourcing in Turkey: Find and Verify the Right Factory

Most import problems start with the first decision: which factory. A polished catalogue says little about real capacity, documents or delivery discipline. Our sourcing service replaces guesswork with a documented shortlist, so you place the first order with evidence instead of hope.

## What you receive

- A shortlist of verified Turkish factories within 5 working days of confirming your brief
- An itemised price comparison on the same terms (EXW or FOB), so offers can be compared line by line
- Sample comparison notes with deviations from your specification marked
- Negotiation support on price, minimum order quantity, payment terms and lead time
- A written summary of risks for each supplier, with our recommendation

You decide who to work with. We do not push a supplier, and we do not hide the weak points of an offer.

## How the process works

1. **Brief (day 1).** You send the product specification, target price, volume, certifications and delivery window. You get a free quotation for our service in 24h.
2. **Search and screening (up to 5 working days).** We search by production capability, not by who advertises the most. Factories that cannot match your specification are removed early.
3. **Verification.** We check trade registration and tax identity, export history, relevant certificates (for example ISO 9001, or CE where your product needs it), and evidence of machinery and capacity. For high-value orders we recommend an on-site visit combined with a [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection).
4. **Samples.** We request samples from the shortlist and compare them against your specification. Sample lead time depends on the product, from days for stock items to several weeks for custom work.
5. **Quotation and contract.** We help you fix the Incoterm, payment terms, quality clause and delay penalties in writing before production starts.
6. **Order follow-up.** We track the production schedule and raise problems early. When the order is large, we add a mid-production check.

## What we need from you

A good brief saves a full round of samples. Please prepare:

- Drawings, photos or a reference product, and any tolerance that matters
- Target price and the Incoterm you want to compare against
- Quantity per order and expected yearly volume
- Required certificates and the destination market
- Packaging and labelling requirements
- Your preferred delivery date and port or address

If some items are unknown, say so. We will help you define them. Incomplete briefs are normal at the start.

## Why buyers source in Turkey

Turkey sits next to Europe and the Middle East, so shipments often travel by road or short sea routes instead of weeks at sea. Industrial goods made in Turkey can move into the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to product scope and rules of origin. Many Turkish factories accept smaller runs than very large producers elsewhere, which helps new product lines and seasonal ranges.

These points help, but they do not replace verification. A good location does not make a weak factory strong. That is why every shortlist passes through the checks above.

## Risks we help you manage

| Risk | What we do |
| :--- | :--- |
| Trading company presented as a factory | Ask for production evidence and verify the registered activity |
| Sample better than mass production | Fix the approved sample as the reference and plan an inspection |
| Hidden costs in the offer | Compare offers on identical terms and list every cost line |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How fast do I get a quotation?
You receive a free quotation for the sourcing service within 24 hours of sending your brief. Send a specification and target volume to make it accurate.

### Can you source products I have never made before?
Yes. Custom products need a clearer brief and usually one extra sample round. We tell you early if the product is unrealistic for the target price.

### Do you guarantee the factory will deliver?
No service can guarantee that. We reduce the risk through verification, written contract terms and order follow-up, and we report problems as soon as we see them.

### What is the minimum order quantity?
It depends on the product and the factory. Stock items can be small, while custom production usually needs a higher quantity. The shortlist shows each factory's real minimum.

### Do I need to visit Turkey?
Not necessarily. Samples, photos, video calls and an on-site inspection by our team can replace a first visit. We can organise a visit if you want one.

### Can you also handle quality control and shipping?
Yes. Sourcing connects to [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection), [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing), [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics) and [export customs documents](/en/services/customs), so one team can follow your order from supplier to port.
