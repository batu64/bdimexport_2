## What Our Dual-Hub China Sourcing Includes

1. **Direct Factory Discovery & Screening:** We filter out trading companies posing as factories by verifying business licenses, tax registration, export permits, and production floor capabilities.
2. **Side-by-Side Landed Cost Modeling:** Instead of comparing raw EXW/FOB quotes blindly, we calculate ocean freight, destination tariffs, and inspection costs so you compare true landed cost per unit.
3. **Sample Consolidation:** When testing 3 to 5 Chinese suppliers, we collect samples at one hub, inspect dimensions beforehand, and consolidate into a single international courier shipment to save express freight cost.
4. **Production Follow-Up & Quality Gate:** Every order includes milestone tracking and mandatory [pre-shipment inspection](/en/services/inspection) before final balance release.
