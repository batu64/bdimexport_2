# B2B Commercial Intelligence, Due Diligence and Trade Reports

International trade losses rarely stem from a lack of available suppliers; they happen when buyers wire deposits or sign exclusive distribution agreements based on incomplete information. Catalogs, website badges, and trade-show booths can all be rented. Legal registries, customs HS-code trade flows, and verified production capacity cannot.

Bdimexport Commercial Intelligence delivers **evidence-backed corporate verification, HS-code market analysis, and landed-cost intelligence reports** for importers and exporters operating across Türkiye, China, Europe, and North America.

## Three Core Intelligence Deliverables

| Report Module | Data Verified & Analyzed | Strategic Decision Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1. Supplier Due Diligence Report | Official corporate registry, capital, beneficial ownership, export license, litigation & physical site check | Prevents wire fraud and filters out shell brokers before deposit payment |
| 2. Landed Cost & Tariff Feasibility | HS Code classification, bilateral tariffs (ATR / FTA / MFN), compliance tests (CE / FDA / TAREKS) and freight modeling | Reveals exact unit cost delivered to your warehouse with zero hidden surprises |
| 3. Export Market & Buyer Intelligence | Import volume trends by country, unit price benchmarks, competitor gaps and verified active importer profiles | Pinpoints high-margin markets and real buyers ready for outreach |

## Built for Global Importers and Manufacturers Alike
