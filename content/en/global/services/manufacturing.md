# Private Label Manufacturing in Turkey: Your Brand, Their Production Line

Building a product line from zero is slow when you manage every factory conversation yourself. Our manufacturing service connects your brand idea with Turkish OEM and ODM producers and manages the path from specification to the first shipment. You keep the brand and the customer. We keep the project moving.

## OEM, ODM and private label: which one fits you

| Model | How it works | Best for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Private label (ready product) | You choose an existing product and sell it under your brand | Fast launch, low development cost |
| OEM | The factory builds your design or specification | Buyers with their own product definition |
| ODM | The factory designs the product, you add brand and changes | Buyers who want a new range without a design team |

Many projects start with private label to test the market and move to OEM or ODM once volume grows. We help you pick the model that matches your budget, volume and timeline.

## How the project runs

1. **Brief and feasibility (24h to first answer).** You describe the product, target retail or wholesale price, volume and market. We tell you quickly whether the target is realistic.
2. **Manufacturer matching.** We select producers that already make similar products and verify their documents, as described in our [sourcing service](/en/services/sourcing).
3. **Development and samples.** The factory prepares samples. We compare them with your specification and record every change request. Each approved sample becomes a signed reference.
4. **Pre-production approval.** Before the full run, you approve materials, colours, packaging artwork and the final specification. Approval is written, not verbal.
5. **Production.** We follow the schedule and plan quality checks during production. See [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) for the standard we use.
6. **Final inspection and shipment.** Goods are checked against the approved sample before they leave the factory, then handed to [logistics](/en/services/logistics).

## Packaging and labelling for your market

Packaging is where many private label projects stumble. Each destination market has its own label content, language and warning rules. We ask for your market requirements at the start, build them into the packaging specification and check them at inspection. We cannot replace your own compliance review, so for regulated products please confirm the final label with your local expert.

## Protecting your brand and design

Before sharing drawings or brand assets, we recommend a confidentiality agreement with the factory. We also help you write down who owns tooling, moulds and artwork, because this question causes trouble later if it stays unwritten. We are not a law firm, so important contracts should be reviewed by your own legal adviser.

## Minimum order and costs

Minimum order quantities depend on the product, the material and the factory. Private label items are usually more flexible than fully custom products. ## Common problems and how we handle them

| Problem | Our approach |
| :--- | :--- |
| Mass production differs from the sample | Sign a reference sample and inspect against it |
| Artwork prints with wrong colours | Approve a physical proof, not only a screen file |
| Factory raises price after sampling | Fix price conditions in writing before development starts |
| Product fails a market test | Agree required tests up front and run them on pre-production samples |
| Schedule slips | Set milestones and review them weekly |

## Communication during the project

Private label projects involve many small decisions, and unanswered questions cost time. We keep one shared list of open decisions, with who must answer and by when. After each sample round you receive a short summary: what was approved, what must change and what the factory promised. When a decision is yours, such as a colour or a material choice, we explain the options, the cost effect and the effect on the schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I start with a small quantity?
Often yes for private label products, because the factory already makes them. Custom OEM products usually have a higher minimum. We show the real minimums during matching.

### Who owns the design and the moulds?
It depends on what you agree. We recommend writing ownership of designs, tooling and artwork into the contract before development begins.

### How long does development take?
It varies by product. A relabelled stock item can be quick, while a new design needs several sample rounds. You receive a dated development plan within 5 working days.

### Can you handle packaging design?
We prepare packaging specifications and coordinate with the factory and your designer. Final artwork stays under your control and requires your written approval.

### What if my product needs certificates?
Tell us the destination market. We ask the factory for existing certificates and plan any additional testing before mass production.

### Do you also arrange quality control and shipping?
Yes. Manufacturing connects to [inspection](/en/services/inspection), [logistics](/en/services/logistics) and [export customs documents](/en/services/customs).
