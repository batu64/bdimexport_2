# Turkish Export Customs Clearance: Documents That Let Your Goods Leave on Time

Customs is rarely the reason you buy from Turkey, but it is a common reason a shipment stops. A wrong tariff code, a missing certificate or an invoice that does not match the packing list can hold a container at the border. Our customs service prepares and checks the export documents before the goods move, so that paperwork does not become the delay.

## What you receive

- A document checklist for your product and destination within 24h of receiving your order details
- Review of the commercial invoice, packing list and transport document for consistency
- Tariff code (HS code) check for the export declaration
- Coordination with a licensed customs broker for the electronic export declaration
- Help with origin documents and certificates when your destination or product requires them
- A complete, organised document set for your records

## Documents we typically prepare or check

| Document | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Commercial invoice | Declares value, terms of sale and parties; must match the order |
| Packing list | Lists packages, weights and contents; must match the goods |
| Export declaration | Official customs declaration lodged electronically through a broker |
| Transport document | CMR for road, bill of lading for sea, air waybill for air |
| Certificate of origin | Proves where the goods were made; issued through the chamber of commerce |
| Movement or preferential origin proof | For example ATR for industrial goods moving to the European Union, or EUR.1 where the rules require it |
| Product-specific certificates | Examples: health or phytosanitary certificates for food and plants, test or conformity documents |

Which preferential proof or certificate applies depends on the product, the destination and the rules of origin. We check each case instead of using one fixed template.

## How the process works

1. **Order details (day 1).** You send the product description, value, destination and the Incoterm. We return a document checklist in 24h.
2. **Classification check.** We verify the HS code that the exporter intends to use and flag doubtful descriptions before they reach the declaration.
3. **Document review.** We compare the invoice, packing list and transport document for matching quantities, values, parties and terms.
4. **Declaration with a broker.** A licensed customs broker lodges the electronic export declaration. We supply the verified data and follow the status.
5. **Origin and special documents.** We help request certificates from the relevant authorities and chambers, and we check that the details match the commercial documents.
6. **Release and handover.** After release, we hand the final document set to you and to the carrier. See our [logistics service](/en/services/logistics) for the shipment itself.

## Common mistakes we prevent

- Different product descriptions on the invoice and the packing list
- Quantities or weights that do not match between documents
- A tariff code that does not reflect the actual product
- Missing or expired product certificates
- A certificate of origin that names the wrong consignee or goods
- An Incoterm in the contract that does not match the documents

Each of these can delay a shipment. Most can be found in a short review before the goods leave the factory.

## Who does what

The seller is normally the exporter of record in Turkey, and the licensed broker lodges the declaration. We do not replace the broker or the exporter. We work between you, the factory, the broker and the carrier so that everyone uses the same correct data. Import clearance, duties and taxes at your destination follow your own country rules and are handled by your importer or broker. We can coordinate with them, but we cannot give legal or tax advice for your destination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How fast do I get the document checklist?
You receive a checklist for your product and destination within 24 hours of sending complete order details.

### Do you act as the customs broker?
We coordinate the process with a licensed customs broker who lodges the export declaration. This keeps the declaration with a party that holds the legal authorisation.

### Do my goods need a certificate of origin?
It depends on your destination, your buyer and the terms of the sale. Some buyers and letters of credit require it. We tell you whether it applies when we review your order.

### What is the difference between ATR and EUR.1?
Both are origin or movement proofs used in different trade regimes. Which one applies depends on the product and the destination, so we check the rule for your goods before the shipment.

### Can you help if documents are already wrong?
Yes. Tell us as early as possible. Some errors can be corrected before the declaration, while later corrections take more time and may involve fees.

### Do you handle import customs in my country?
We coordinate with your broker or importer, but import duties, taxes and legal advice stay with them. We help make sure the export documents support a smooth import.
