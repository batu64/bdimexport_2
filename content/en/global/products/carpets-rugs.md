## Why Source Carpets and Rugs from Turkey

A rug is judged by colour, feel and how it lies on the floor. Turkish producers combine a long rug-making tradition with modern weaving, and offer many materials, sizes and qualities for retail and project buyers.

- **Tradition and modern production.** Turkey has a long rug-making tradition, and modern weaving mills now produce large volumes with many design options.
- **Many materials and qualities.** Producers work with wool, acrylic, polypropylene and other yarns, in different pile heights and densities.
- **Design flexibility.** Manufacturers can produce your own designs and sizes, and many offer collections for retail and project use.
- **Reach to nearby markets.** Road and sea transport serve Europe and neighbouring regions, with rolled or folded packing for efficient loading.

### What to Prepare Before You Request a Quotation

- Design files or reference rugs, with colours and sizes

### Typical Order Terms

Rug prices depend on material, density, size, design and quantity. Machine-made rugs in catalogue designs can be ordered in smaller quantities, while custom designs need loom set-up and larger runs. Handmade rugs take longer and are priced per piece. We ask producers to quote on a clear basis such as EXW or FOB and to state what the price includes. Colour approval uses physical samples, because screens do not show colour accurately. Payment terms are agreed in writing, normally with a deposit and a balance linked to inspection. Lead time depends on the loom schedule. For larger orders, we agree how rugs are numbered and packed by design and size, so that your warehouse can pick them quickly, and we keep an approved sample of each design to compare with repeat orders.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Rugs ship rolled or folded in protective wrap, in cartons or bales. Tight packing saves freight, but it must not leave permanent creases. We check wrapping and labels, protect goods against moisture and arrange container or truck transport.

## Quality Control and Certification

A rug is judged by touch, appearance and how it wears.

- **Specification:** size tolerance, pile height, density, weight and material against the approved sample.
- **Appearance:** colour match, pattern accuracy, shading differences, loose threads and flaws.
- **Performance:** shedding, colourfastness and backing quality; flammability requirements may apply in your market.
- **Packing:** rolled or folded with protective wrap, clean cartons or bales and labels with size and quality.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a measured, photographed report within 48 hours of the visit. For brand ranges see [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).

### What We Check at Inspection

- Size, pile height and weight against the approved sample
- Colour match, pattern accuracy and shading differences
- Edges, fringes, backing and loose threads
- Labels, care instructions and barcodes
- Rolling or folding method, wrapping and carton or bale marking
- Loading plan to avoid crushing and moisture

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I order my own design?
Yes. Many producers can weave your design in different sizes and colourways. Allow time for development samples.

### What is the difference between machine-made and handmade?
Machine-made rugs are produced on looms in volume, while handmade rugs are knotted or hand-tufted, take longer and cost more per piece.

### How do you control colour?
We approve a physical sample and compare production rugs against it, with attention to shade differences between batches.

### What is the minimum order?
It depends on the design and the loom set-up. Catalogue designs can start lower, while custom designs need larger runs.

### What materials are available?
Wool, acrylic, polypropylene and blends are common. The material affects feel, durability and price, so define it in the order.

### How are rugs shipped?
Rolled or folded in wrap, in cartons or bales, by container or truck. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I order custom sizes?
Many producers offer custom sizes, with limits set by the loom width. Define the sizes and tolerance in the brief.

### How do I clean and care for the rugs?
Care depends on the material. Ask the producer for care instructions and include them on the label.

### Do you source runners and outdoor rugs?
Producers offer runners and outdoor materials. Tell us the use and size range so we can match the right quality.
