## Why Source CNC Machinery from Turkey

A CNC machine is a long-term investment, so buyers need confidence in the builder, the machine and the support. Turkey has an industrial base that uses machine tools every day, and builders who customise machines and support customers in nearby markets.

- **A local industrial base.** Turkish machine builders serve automotive, white goods and metalworking industries in the region. They are used to building to customer specifications.
- **Customisation and service.** Many builders adapt machines to your parts, and offer training and commissioning. Local engineering teams can respond quickly to questions.
- **Short routes to Europe.** Heavy machinery ships by road or sea in short transit times, which simplifies delivery planning and installation.
- **Customs union with the EU.** Industrial goods made in Turkey can enter the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to rules of origin. CE marking and other product rules still apply.

### What to Prepare Before You Request a Quotation

- Typical parts, materials and sizes, with drawings
- Required accuracy, surface finish and production volume

- Control system, software and tooling requirements
- Power supply, space limits and operating environment

### Typical Order Terms

Machine tools are priced on the configuration, so the offer must list the base machine, options, tooling, software and accessories as separate items. Payment is usually split into milestones, such as a deposit at order, a payment at the factory acceptance test and a balance at delivery or commissioning. Delivery time depends on the machine and the order book. We help you write the acceptance criteria, warranty terms, spare parts availability and training scope into the contract so that the offer and the delivered machine match. Installation and commissioning are quoted as a separate scope.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Machines ship by truck or container, depending on size and weight. Lifting points, lashing and protection against moisture are part of the packing plan. We confirm dimensions and weight early so that transport and handling at your site can be planned.

## Quality Control and Certification

A machine is only as good as its acceptance test. We help you define what will be checked and who witnesses it.

- **Specification:** axis travel, spindle, accuracy and repeatability, control system and accessories against the contract.
- **Acceptance test:** a cutting test on your part or a standard test piece, run before shipment, with a signed protocol.
- **Safety and conformity:** CE marking and a declaration of conformity for the EU, and equivalent marks for other markets. Check the rules that apply to your destination.
- **Documentation and support:** manuals, spare parts lists, training, commissioning and warranty terms in writing.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) and acceptance witnessing. The report follows within 48 hours of the visit. Technical experts can join when the machine is complex.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Machine configuration against the signed specification and options list
- Axis travel, spindle or laser data and control version
- Test piece cut or run, with measurements and a signed protocol
- Safety guards, emergency stops and electrical cabinet
- Documentation: manuals, drawings, parts list and declaration of conformity
- Packing, lifting points, lashing and protection against moisture

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Turkish builders customise a machine for my parts?
Many can. Send drawings and requirements, and the builder proposes a configuration. We compare offers on the same technical basis.

### How do I judge whether a builder is reliable?
Check references, test facilities, years of operation and service capacity. We verify these points and can arrange a factory visit or an inspection.

### What is a factory acceptance test?
It is a test at the builder's factory that proves the machine meets the specification before it ships. You agree the test and the pass criteria in the contract.

### What about spare parts and service?
Ask for a spare parts list, response times and training in writing. We include these points in the comparison and the contract.

### What documents do I need to import a machine?
Typically an invoice, packing list, transport document, origin proof and conformity documents. We help check them. See [export customs documents](/en/services/customs).

### How are machines shipped?
By truck or container, depending on size and weight. Packing and lifting instructions are part of the order. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I visit the factory?
Yes. Visits are recommended for high-value machines. If you cannot travel, our engineer can witness tests and report with photographs and measurements.

### What payment structure is typical?
Milestone payments are common, for example a deposit, a payment at acceptance and a balance at delivery. Terms are agreed in the contract.

### Do I need local approvals for the control cabinet?
Some markets require electrical conformity of the machine and the cabinet. Tell us your destination and we ask the builder for matching documents.
