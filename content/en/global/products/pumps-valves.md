## Quality Control and Certification

For pumps and valves, documents and tests carry the proof of quality.

- **Material certificates:** a certificate such as EN 10204 type 3.1 links the material to the batch, and should be requested for pressure-bearing parts.
- **Pressure and performance tests:** hydrostatic and seat leakage tests for valves, and performance curves for pumps, with a signed protocol.
- **Conformity:** CE marking, including pressure equipment rules where they apply, and ATEX for explosive atmospheres. Confirm what applies to your destination.
- **Markings and packing:** body markings, nameplates and protective packing for flanges and threads.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) and test witnessing. The report follows within 48 hours of the visit, with photographs and measurements.

- Product type, size, pressure rating and connections against the order
- Body markings, nameplates and heat numbers linked to certificates

- Pressure, leakage or performance test witnessed or checked on records

- Protective caps, packing and crating

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I order valves to a specific standard?
Yes. Name the standard, pressure rating, size and material in the order. The manufacturer confirms it in writing, and we check the marking and test reports.

### What is a 3.1 material certificate?
It is a document from the manufacturer that states the chemical and mechanical values of the material batch used. It is common for pressure parts.

### How are pumps tested?
Pumps are tested for flow, head and sometimes power consumption, using the method agreed in your order. A witnessed test gives you the data before shipment.

### Are small orders possible?
Standard products can ship in smaller quantities, while special materials or designs need larger runs. The shortlist shows each manufacturer's minimum.

### What documentation is included?
Typically a datasheet, test report, material certificates, installation instructions and conformity documents. Agree the set in the order.

### How are pumps and valves shipped?
Crated or palletised, with protection for flanges and threads. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I order to my own drawings?
Yes, many manufacturers cast and machine to customer drawings. Tooling and minimums apply, and we help compare offers.

### Do manufacturers offer stainless steel and special alloys?
Many do. State the grade and standard in the order, and we ask for the material certificate.

### How do you verify performance claims?
Through a witnessed test at the factory or an independent laboratory, using the method you name in the order.
