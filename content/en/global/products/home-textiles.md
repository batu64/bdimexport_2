## Why Source Home Textiles from Turkey

Home textiles are judged by touch, size and durability, and customers remember the first wash. Turkey offers integrated mills and finishing plants, experienced packers for retail and hotel orders, and short routes to European and regional markets.

- **Integrated production.** Spinning, weaving or knitting, dyeing and finishing often sit close together. This makes it easier to control fabric quality, shade and delivery time for towels and bedding.
- **Retail and hospitality experience.** Many factories already supply both retail packs and bulk hotel orders. They understand packaging for shelves, e-commerce and contract deliveries.
- **Fast replenishment.** Road transport to Europe and short sea routes to the Mediterranean region support re-orders and seasonal peaks without long ocean transit.
- **Customs union with the EU.** Industrial goods made in Turkey can enter the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to rules of origin. Please check the details for your product and destination.

## Working with home textile manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Specification.** Define fibre content, weight per square metre, thread count or towel weight, colours, sizes and packaging. Photos or a reference product help when words are not enough.
2. **Factory matching.** We match your product with factories that already make it, then verify their documents, capacity and references.
3. **Sampling and testing.** Samples are checked for weight, size, colour and feel. For towels we also look at absorbency and pile stability. Shrinkage and colour fastness are tested when relevant.
4. **Production follow-up.** We follow the schedule and check the first finished pieces. A mid-production check is useful for large orders and new factories.
5. **Inspection and packing.** We inspect finished goods for size, weight, stitching, stains and packing. Labels, care symbols and fibre content must match your approved artwork.

### What to Prepare Before You Request a Quotation

- Product types, sizes and set composition, for example duvet cover, sheet and pillowcases
- Fibre content and weight, thread count or towel weight
- Colours, designs, embroidery or prints, with reference images
- Labels, care symbols, barcodes and packaging type
- Target market and any standards or certificates your customers expect
- Quantity per design and size, and the delivery window
- Target price and the Incoterm you want to compare offers on

### Typical Order Terms

Minimum quantities depend on the product and the process. Plain products in standard colours are usually easier to order in smaller runs than custom designs or dyed colours. We ask factories to quote fabric, making-up, packing and any printing or embroidery as clear items. For gift sets and retail boxes, artwork and packaging samples are part of the approval process. Payment terms are agreed in writing, normally with a deposit and a balance linked to inspection. Seasonal peaks affect lead time, so we ask for dated production plans.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Home textiles are bulky but light, so packing density drives freight cost. Compression packing saves space but must not crease products or damage retail boxes. We agree the packing method in the order and check cartons and moisture protection before loading.

## Quality Control and Certification

Home textile quality is easy to feel and easy to complain about. Customers notice softness, shrinkage and colour change after the first wash.

- **Specification checks:** weight, dimensions, thread count or towel weight, and fibre content against your order.
- **Performance tests:** shrinkage after washing, colour fastness and, for towels, absorbency.
- **Chemical safety:** an OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certificate or equivalent test report is common. Organic claims need a recognised certificate such as GOTS.
- **Packing and labels:** fibre content, care symbols and barcodes must be correct and readable.

We carry out [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a measured, photographed report within 48 hours of the visit. For brand launches we recommend a pre-shipment inspection on every order.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Dimensions and weight against the approved sample
- Fibre content documents and thread count or towel weight
- Stitching, hems, corners and embroidery quality
- Colour match, stains, holes and loose threads
- Labels, care symbols and barcodes on each product
- Retail packaging, gift boxes and carton marking

### Common Risks and How We Manage Them

| Risk | How we manage it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Shrinkage and colour change after washing | Test shrinkage and colour fastness on samples and set limits in the order. |
| Weight or size below specification | Check weight and dimensions on finished goods against the approved sample. |
| Shade differences between lots | Approve colour standards and keep lots together in packing. |
| Wrong or missing labels | Approve label and care text in writing and check at inspection. |
| Moisture damage in transit | Use sealed inner bags and dry cartons, and plan the container loading. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I order a mix of products from one factory?
Often yes, if the factory makes several product types. A mixed order can reduce freight cost, but each product may have its own minimum. We confirm this during factory matching.

### What certificates do home textiles need?
It depends on your market and customers. Buyers commonly ask for OEKO-TEX Standard 100 or equivalent test reports, and GOTS for organic claims. We check which documents the factory already holds.

### How do you check towel quality?
We compare weight, size and feel with the approved sample, and we test absorbency and shrinkage when they matter for your customer. Results appear in the inspection report.

### Can I develop my own design?
Yes. Factories can weave or print your design. Allow time for development samples and colour approvals. See [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).

### What packaging is available?
Retail boxes, poly bags, hangers and bulk bales are all common. You can also request e-commerce packing. Artwork stays under your control and requires your written approval.

### How long does delivery take?
Production time depends on the product and the season, and transport depends on your destination. Your quotation lists the production lead time and the expected transit time.

### Can I order gift sets or complete bedding sets?
Yes. Many factories make sets with matching pieces and retail packaging. Define set contents, packing and barcode requirements in the brief.

### How do you check bedding quality?
We check fibre content documents, weight, dimensions, stitching, colour and packing against your approved sample. Laboratory tests are available for shrinkage and colour fastness.

### Can factories supply hotel or hospitality textiles?
Many do. Hotel orders focus on durability and consistent quality across large quantities. Tell us the volume and performance requirements.
