## Working with technical fabric manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Define the application.** State what the material must do: filter, protect, reinforce, insulate or cover. Add weight, width, strength, coating, colour and any standard or test method.
2. **Producer matching.** We match your application with producers that already make similar materials, and we verify documents, capacity and references.
3. **Samples and lab testing.** We request samples and compare them with your target values. Independent laboratory tests can confirm weight, strength and other properties.
4. **Production and batch control.** We follow the schedule and check that each batch keeps the approved properties. Batch numbers and test certificates should come with every delivery.
5. **Inspection and packing.** We check roll length, width, defects, labelling and packing before shipment. Rolls need protection against moisture and damage.

### What to Prepare Before You Request a Quotation

- The application and the properties it must meet
- Weight, width, thickness and tolerance
- Strength, elongation, permeability or other performance values
- Coating, treatment, colour and finishing requirements
- Standards or test methods that apply to your market
- Roll length, core size, packing and labelling needs
- Quantity per order, expected yearly volume and delivery window

### Typical Order Terms

Technical fabrics are usually sold by roll length or weight. Standard products can be ordered in smaller quantities, while custom coatings, colours or constructions need larger runs and development time. We ask producers to quote on a clear basis and to name the test methods behind the datasheet values. Batch certificates and retained samples are agreed before the first order, because repeat orders must match the first delivery. Payment terms are agreed in writing, and lead time depends on whether the material is in stock or made to order.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Rolls are heavy and sensitive to moisture and crushing. They ship wrapped on cores, on pallets or in cartons, and the packing instruction is part of the order. We check labels and wrapping before loading and plan the container so that rolls do not move.

## Quality Control and Certification

Technical fabrics are bought on properties, not on appearance, so measured data is essential.

- **Specification:** weight per square metre, width, thickness, strength, elongation and any coating or treatment.
- **Test reports:** results from an independent laboratory using the test method you name, for each batch when the application requires it.
- **Roll checks:** length, width, defects, joints and winding quality.
- **Application standards:** medical, filtration or automotive uses often follow specific standards. Tell us the standard, and we ask the producer for matching documents.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a measured report within 48 hours of the visit. Laboratory tests are organised separately and listed as their own cost line.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Roll length, width and winding quality
- Weight per square metre on sampled rolls
- Visible defects, holes, contamination and joins
- Coating, colour and finish compared with the approved sample
- Roll labels, batch numbers and certificate references
- Wrapping, cores and pallet stability

### Common Risks and How We Manage Them

| Risk | How we manage it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Properties differ from the datasheet | Agree test methods and require independent laboratory tests on samples and batches. |
| Batch-to-batch variation | Require batch certificates and compare each delivery with an approved retained sample. |
| Damage to rolls in transport | Specify core, wrapping and pallet packing and check them before loading. |
| Material not suited to the application | Test a sample in your process before you place a volume order. |
| Wrong width or length on delivery | Check roll labels, width and length during inspection. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you source a material I only know by its properties?
Yes. Send target weight, width, strength and use. We ask producers for matching materials and compare samples with your target values.

### Do producers give test certificates?
Many do. We agree before the order which properties must be tested, with which method, and whether tests are needed per batch.

### What is the minimum order for technical fabrics?
It depends on the material and process. Standard products can ship in smaller quantities, while custom coatings or colours need larger runs.

### How are rolls packed?
Rolls are wound on cores, wrapped and placed in cartons or on pallets. Packing instructions should be part of the order, especially for sensitive materials.

### Can samples be shipped quickly?
Small samples can often be sent by courier. Lead time depends on whether the sample is a standard item or a new development.

### Do you handle export documents?
Yes. We coordinate the commercial invoice, packing list and origin documents with a licensed customs broker. See [export customs documents](/en/services/customs).

### Can producers make custom constructions?
Many can, with development time and minimum quantities. Provide the target properties and the application so the producer can propose a construction.

### How do I compare two fabrics from different producers?
Compare the same properties tested with the same method on the same sample size. We help set up a comparison table.

### Do you work with nonwovens for hygiene or medical use?
Some producers serve these markets. Such uses have strict requirements, so tell us the standard and we ask for matching documents.
