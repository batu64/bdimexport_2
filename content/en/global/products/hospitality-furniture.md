## Working with hospitality furniture manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Project brief and drawings.** Share the designer's drawings, bill of quantities, materials, finishes, fire and durability requirements, and the delivery schedule linked to the opening date.
2. **Manufacturer matching and verification.** We shortlist manufacturers with comparable projects and verify registration, capacity, financial reliability and references.
3. **Mock-up and room sample approval.** The manufacturer builds a mock-up or room sample. You approve materials, finish, dimensions and details in writing before volume production.
4. **Production and phased checks.** We follow production by room type or batch, and inspect during production so that corrections happen early.
5. **Pre-shipment inspection and phased delivery.** We inspect finished goods, plan packing by room or floor and coordinate phased shipment with the installation schedule.

Delivery follows the building schedule, so production and shipping dates are tied to phases. We ask manufacturers for a project plan with approval dates, and we agree a replacement process for damaged or missing items. Warranty terms and spare parts are written into the contract.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Deliveries follow the installation programme, so shipments are grouped by floor or area. Clear labels and packing lists speed up installation. We coordinate production, inspection and shipping dates so that pieces arrive when the site is ready.

## Quality Control and Certification

Contract furniture must look the same across the whole project and survive heavy daily use.

- **Approval standard:** the signed room sample is the reference for materials, finish, dimensions and tolerance.
- **Durability and safety:** strength, stability and wear requirements for non-domestic use, and fire safety requirements of your market. State the standards in the order.
- **Consistency:** finish and colour must match across batches, and parts must interchange.
- **Packing and labelling:** carton marking by room or floor and clear assembly instructions to speed installation.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a measured, photographed report within 48 hours of the visit. For phased projects we can inspect each delivery batch.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Mock-up or approved room sample as the reference standard
- Dimensions, materials and finishes on pieces from each batch
- Strength, stability and function of beds, seating and tables
- Fabric, foam and upholstery quality
- Labelling by room type, floor or phase
- Packing quality and load plan for phased delivery

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can the manufacturer build to the designer's drawings?
Yes, project manufacturers work from drawings and specifications. A mock-up confirms that details and finishes are correct.

### How do you manage deadlines?
We link production and shipping milestones to the installation schedule, follow progress and report delays early. Phased deliveries can reduce risk.

### What about fire and durability standards?
Requirements depend on the country and the building type. Tell us the standards, and we ask the manufacturer for matching test reports.

### Is installation included?
Some manufacturers offer installation or supervision. Agree scope, cost and responsibility in the contract.

### How are large orders packed?
By room or floor, with clear labels and spare parts. This speeds installation and reduces missing items.

### How are the goods shipped?
By container or truck in phases that match the project. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can the manufacturer supply FF&E for a whole hotel?
Large manufacturers can supply a wide range of items, and others cover part of the scope. We shortlist and coordinate suppliers by category.

### How are room mock-ups handled?
The manufacturer builds a mock-up or room sample, you approve it in writing, and production follows that standard.

### Can you coordinate with my designer or contractor?
Yes. We communicate with designers and contractors to keep drawings and approvals aligned.
