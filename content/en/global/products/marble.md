## Why Source Marble and Natural Stone from Turkey

Natural stone is chosen for its look, so colour, vein and finish must match what the project approved. Turkish producers quarry and process many stone types, offer different finishes and formats, and ship from Aegean and Mediterranean ports.

- **Variety of stone types.** Producers offer many colours and patterns in marble, travertine and other natural stone, which gives designers a wide choice.
- **From quarry to finished product.** Many companies control quarrying, cutting, finishing and packing, which helps manage availability and consistency.
- **Finishes and formats.** Polished, honed, brushed and other finishes are available, in blocks, slabs, tiles and cut-to-size products for projects.
- **Routes by sea and land.** Ports on the Aegean and Mediterranean coasts and road links to Europe support heavy cargo delivery.

## Working with marble and natural stone manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Define stone and format.** State stone name or colour, block, slab or tile, size, thickness, finish, grade and quantities. For projects, add the drawings and the installation schedule.
2. **Producer matching and verification.** We shortlist producers with access to the stone you need, and verify registration, quarry or factory capacity and references.
3. **Reference samples and approval.** Natural stone varies. You approve physical samples or reference slabs, and the approved reference defines the range of colour and vein accepted.
4. **Production and batch control.** Slabs and tiles are cut, finished, calibrated and sorted. We check that batches stay within the approved range and keep batch numbers together.
5. **Inspection and loading.** We check size, thickness, finish, defects, shade and packing, and supervise loading. Then we arrange transport and export documents.

### What to Prepare Before You Request a Quotation

- Stone name or colour reference, with photos or physical samples
- Format: block, slab, tile or cut-to-size, with sizes and thickness
- Finish: polished, honed, brushed or other, and edge details
- Grade and acceptable variation in colour, vein and defects
- Quantities per format and the project schedule
- Packing method and loading requirements
- Target price and the Incoterm you want to compare offers on

### Typical Order Terms

Natural stone is priced by type, grade, format, finish and quantity. Block purchases, slabs and tiles have different price bases, such as per cubic metre or per square metre, and the quotation states which one applies. Cut-to-size work adds processing and packing cost. We ask producers to quote on a clear basis such as EXW or FOB, and to state what the price includes. For projects, we recommend reserving the whole quantity from one lot, because later lots of natural stone can look different. Payment terms are agreed in writing, normally with a deposit and a balance linked to inspection.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Stone is heavy, so weight distribution matters. Slabs ship in crates or on A-frames, tiles on pallets. We supervise loading, check bracing and plan the container so that weight is balanced and lots stay together.

## Quality Control and Certification

Natural stone is never identical, so quality control means agreeing the range and holding to it.

- **Reference range:** approve physical samples and photographs of reference slabs, with acceptable variation in colour, vein and pattern.
- **Dimensions and finish:** length, width, thickness tolerance, flatness, surface finish and edge quality.
- **Defects:** cracks, repairs, stains, chips and holes, graded according to the contract.
- **Packing:** crates or bundles with protection between slabs, and a loading plan that avoids breakage.
- **Standards and conformity:** relevant European standards for natural stone products may apply for your market. Tell us which ones you need.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection), including loading supervision. The report follows within 48 hours of the visit.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Slab or tile dimensions, thickness and flatness
- Colour and vein range against the approved reference
- Surface finish, edges, cracks, repairs and stains
- Bundle or crate numbering and lot identification
- Packing: protection between slabs and securing in crates
- Loading: balance, bracing and container condition

### Common Risks and How We Manage Them

| Risk | How we manage it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Colour and vein differ from the sample | Approve a reference range, reserve the whole quantity from one lot and inspect before packing. |
| Cracks or repaired slabs | Define allowed repairs and defects in the contract and inspect slabs. |
| Thickness or size out of tolerance | Set tolerances and measure samples from each bundle. |
| Breakage in transit | Use crates or bundles with inner protection and supervise loading. |
| Mixed lots in one shipment | Label lots and keep them together in the loading plan. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I choose slabs individually?
Often yes for premium projects. You approve photographs or visit the producer, and the selected slabs are marked and packed together.

### How do you keep colour consistent?
We approve a reference range, keep batches together and inspect shade before packing. For large projects we recommend ordering all material in one batch.

### What finishes are available?
Polished, honed, brushed and other finishes are common. Show a sample and the producer confirms what it can match.

### How is natural stone packed?
In crates, bundles or on A-frames, with protection between slabs. Good packing prevents most breakage in transit.

### What is the minimum order?
It depends on the stone and the format. Stock tiles can ship in smaller quantities, while block purchases or custom projects are larger.

### How is stone shipped?
By sea in containers or breakbulk, or by road for nearby destinations. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I buy blocks for my own factory?
Yes. Blocks are sold with agreed dimensions and quality grades. Inspection at the quarry or yard checks the blocks before purchase.

### What about cut-to-size and project orders?
Producers cut stone to drawings for floors, walls and stairs. We recommend drawings with numbering and a packing list per area.

### Is travertine different from marble?
They are different stone types with different appearance and technical properties. A producer can explain suitability for floors, walls or outdoor use.
