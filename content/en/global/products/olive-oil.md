## Why Source Olive Oil from Turkey

Olive oil is a product where origin, grade and freshness matter to buyers and to the end consumer. Turkish producers supply both bulk oil for blending and bottled oil under private label, with groves and mills close to the Aegean and Mediterranean ports.

- **Olive groves near the coast.** Olive groves along the Aegean and Mediterranean coasts supply many producers, and the harvest takes place in autumn and winter. Buyers can often visit regions and mills within a short trip.
- **Bulk and packed options.** Producers offer bulk shipments in drums, IBCs or flexitanks as well as bottles, cans and tins under your brand. This lets you choose between blending and retail-ready supply.
- **Short routes to Europe and the Middle East.** Road and short sea routes help deliver within a reasonable time, which matters for a product that should reach shelves with a long shelf life.
- **Private label experience.** Many packers already fill for foreign brands. They know label rules, glass and tin packaging and pallet requirements.

### Typical Order Terms

Olive oil is priced by grade, crop year, packaging and quantity, and prices move with the harvest. Bulk contracts name the grade, the analysis parameters, the sampling method and the tolerance on quantity. Bottled oil adds packaging, label and pallet costs, and artwork must be approved before printing. We ask producers to quote on a clear basis such as EXW or FOB and to state what is included. Payment terms are agreed in writing, often with a deposit and a balance against documents, and the contract names who bears the cost of laboratory analysis.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Bottled oil ships in cartons on pallets, and bulk oil ships in drums, IBCs or flexitanks. Heat and light damage olive oil, so we plan loading and transport to limit exposure. Documents such as the analysis certificate travel with the shipment, and we check them before dispatch.

## Quality Control and Certification

Olive oil is judged by chemistry and by taste, and a label cannot replace analysis.

- **Grade and parameters:** the quality grade is defined by international and EU standards. Ask for analysis results for the parameters stated in your contract.
- **Sensory evaluation:** extra virgin olive oil also requires a tasting panel result. Request it for each batch you buy.
- **Food safety:** look for certificates such as ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, BRCGS or IFS, and organic certification where you sell it as organic.
- **Traceability:** batch numbers, harvest year and the producer's records should match the label and the documents.

We organise [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection), sampling for independent laboratory tests and loading supervision. The inspection report follows within 48 hours of the visit. Laboratory costs are listed separately.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Sealed samples taken at the producer for laboratory analysis
- Packaging type, closures, tins or bottles, and cleanliness
- Label content, batch number, best-before date and language
- Quantity: number of cartons, pallets or tank volume
- Storage conditions: temperature, light and tank condition
- Pallet stability, cartons and loading conditions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I buy olive oil in bulk and bottle it myself?
Yes. Bulk supply in drums, IBCs or flexitanks is common. We help you set quality parameters, sampling and the delivery contract.

### How do I know the oil is extra virgin?
Request an independent laboratory analysis and a sensory panel result for the batch. We arrange sampling and tests so that the result refers to the oil you actually buy.

### Does the producer offer private label?
Many do. You provide the design and label text, the producer fills and packs. We check artwork and finished packaging against your approval.

### When is the harvest and when can I order?
The harvest takes place in autumn and winter, and producers sell the new crop afterwards. You can order throughout the year, but harvest year and stock levels affect price and availability.

### What documents come with shipments?
Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin when needed, analysis certificate and transport document. We help check them before shipment. See [export customs documents](/en/services/customs).

### How is olive oil shipped?
Bottled oil ships in cartons on pallets, and bulk oil ships in drums, IBCs or flexitanks. Protect the oil from heat and light. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### What packaging sizes are available for bottled oil?
Common formats include glass bottles and tins in several sizes. Tell us the sizes your customers prefer and the producer confirms what it can fill.

### Can I buy olive pomace oil or flavoured oils?
Some producers offer other oil types and flavoured products. Send your product list and we check availability and documents.

### Do you help with tasting and approval?
We organise samples and can arrange laboratory and sensory analysis. You approve the sample before the order, and we compare shipped lots with it.
