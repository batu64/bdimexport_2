## Working with ceramic tile manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Define the tile.** State type (wall, floor, outdoor), material, size, thickness, surface, edge, shade range and the use class. Add quantity and delivery schedule.
2. **Producer matching.** We shortlist producers that offer the collection or format you need and verify registration, certificates and capacity.

4. **Production and batch control.** Tiles are fired in batches, and shade and size can vary between batches. We follow batch codes and ensure that a project receives matched batches.
5. **Inspection and loading.** We check size, flatness, surface defects, shade, packing and labels, and supervise loading to reduce breakage.

Tiles are heavy and breakable. Pallets with corner protection and strong wrapping reduce damage, and careful loading prevents shifting. We check shade and calibre codes, supervise loading when asked, and arrange sea or road transport.

## Quality Control and Certification

Tile problems usually come from shade and size variation, and they are expensive to fix after installation.

- **Dimensions and flatness:** size tolerance, thickness, straightness, squareness and surface flatness, including for rectified tiles.
- **Shade and batch:** tiles carry batch and shade codes, and a project should receive matched codes. Approve a shade variation range before production.
- **Technical performance:** water absorption, breaking strength, slip resistance and frost resistance are tested according to recognised standards such as the ISO 10545 series. Ask for test reports for your tile.
- **Packing:** boxes, pallets and wrapping that protect edges and corners, and clear labels with size, shade, batch and quantity.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a measured, photographed report within 48 hours of the visit, and loading supervision when requested.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Size, thickness, squareness and flatness on sampled boxes
- Shade and calibre codes on boxes against the order
- Surface defects, glaze and pattern under normal light
- Box labels: design, size, shade, batch and quantity
- Pallet build, wrapping and corner protection
- Loading plan and container condition

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you avoid shade differences?
Tiles of the same batch and shade code are packed together, and we check codes before loading. For large projects, order the whole quantity from one production run if possible.

### What is a rectified tile?
A rectified tile has edges cut after firing to give precise size, which allows narrow joints. The specification should state the tolerance.

### What tests are important?
Water absorption, breaking strength, abrasion or slip resistance and frost resistance, depending on where the tile is used. Ask for test reports under recognised standards.

### Can I add trims and matching accessories?
Yes, many collections include trims, steps and decorative pieces. Order them with the main tiles so that shades match.

### What about breakage in transit?
Good packing and careful loading reduce breakage. We suggest a small extra quantity for cuts and damage, and we check packing before loading.

### How are tiles shipped?
Palletised in containers or by truck. Weight is high, so plan the load carefully. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### How much extra should I order for cuts and breakage?
Installers usually order a margin for cuts and damage. Ask your installer how much is needed for the layout and add it to the first order.

### Can I order a custom design?
Some manufacturers produce custom designs for large orders. Allow time for development and approvals.

### What documents come with tiles?
Usually an invoice, packing list, certificate of origin when required and test reports if agreed. We check them before shipment.
