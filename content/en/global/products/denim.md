## Why Source Denim from Turkey

Denim buyers care about fit, wash and consistency across thousands of pieces. Turkey offers jeans factories with their own laundries, denim mills nearby and short routes to European retailers, which supports fast cycles and re-orders.

- **Fabric and garment in one country.** Denim mills, jeans factories and laundries are available close to each other. This shortens development time and makes it easier to control fabric, fit and wash quality.
- **Wash and finishing know-how.** Laundry and finishing processes decide the look of jeans. Many Turkish producers run their own washing units and offer a wide range of effects.
- **Close to European retailers.** Short transport distances help fast fashion cycles and re-orders. Road freight is common for Europe.
- **Customs union with the EU.** Industrial goods made in Turkey can enter the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to rules of origin. Please check the details for your product.

## Working with denim manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Fabric and fit definition.** Decide fabric weight, composition, stretch, colour and fit. Send a reference pair if you have one. Fit is defined by a measurement table and a size set.
2. **Factory and mill matching.** We match you with jeans factories and, when needed, denim mills, then verify documents, capacity and references.
3. **Development samples and washes.** The factory makes fit samples and wash samples. We compare shade, abrasion, whiskers, hardware and measurements, and record each change.
4. **Production and wash control.** Cutting, sewing, washing and finishing follow the approved reference. We check wash consistency between batches, because shade drift is a common issue.
5. **Final inspection.** We inspect against the approved sample with AQL sampling, including measurements after washing, labels, hardware and packing.

### What to Prepare Before You Request a Quotation

- Reference jeans or tech pack with measurement table and fit name
- Fabric weight, composition, stretch and colour, or a fabric reference
- Wash and finish references, with photos of the desired effects
- Trims: buttons, rivets, zippers, labels, patches and pocket bags
- Size range, quantity per style and wash
- Packaging, hangtag and barcode requirements
- Target price, delivery window and destination market

### Typical Order Terms

Denim minimums depend on the fabric and the wash. Using fabric the factory already holds lowers the minimum, while custom fabric needs larger runs. Wash development adds sampling time, so plan extra weeks for new washes. We ask for quotes that list fabric, cut and sew, wash, trims and packing separately. Payment terms are agreed in writing, usually with a deposit and a balance linked to inspection. Because shade can drift between wash batches, we agree how batches will be compared before production starts.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Jeans usually ship folded in cartons, sorted by style, wash and size. A clear carton marking system saves time at your warehouse. We check the packing list against cartons and arrange road freight for Europe or sea and air freight for other markets.

## Quality Control and Certification

For denim, the details decide whether a pair of jeans looks premium or cheap.

- **Fabric:** weight, composition, stretch and recovery, shrinkage and colour fastness (crocking).
- **Construction:** stitch density, seam strength, pocket and rivet attachment, and zipper or button function.
- **Washes:** shade match to the approved wash, abrasion pattern and consistency between pieces.
- **Chemical compliance:** restricted substance rules such as REACH apply for the EU, and many retailers ask for test reports or a certificate such as OEKO-TEX Standard 100.

We carry out [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) and send a photographed, measured report within 48 hours of the visit. For fashion washes we recommend approving a physical wash standard, and keeping one copy at the factory and one with you.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Measurements before and after washing against the size chart
- Wash shade, abrasion and whiskers compared with the wash standard
- Seam strength, stitch density and bar tacks at stress points
- Rivets, buttons and zippers: attachment and function
- Labels, patches, hangtags and barcodes
- Packing, folding and carton marking by size and wash

### Common Risks and How We Manage Them

| Risk | How we manage it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Wash shade differs between batches | Approve a physical wash standard and compare batches against it before packing. |
| Shrinkage and twisting after washing | Test shrinkage on samples and check measurements after wash on production pieces. |
| Weak seams or hardware | Check seam strength, rivet and button attachment and zipper function in inspection. |
| Fabric different from the sample | Document fabric weight and composition and compare delivered fabric with the sample. |
| Chemical compliance of washes | Ask for test reports on restricted substances and confirm the laundry's process controls. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I order jeans with my own fit and wash?
Yes. Send a reference pair or a measurement table. The factory develops fit and wash samples, and you approve each stage in writing.

### What is the usual minimum order for jeans?
It depends on the factory, fabric and wash. Using the factory's stock fabric can lower the minimum. Custom fabric and special washes need larger runs. We show real minimums in the shortlist.

### How do you control wash consistency?
We approve a wash standard, compare batches against it during production and inspect finished pieces for shade and abrasion. Differences are recorded with photographs.

### Do you work with sustainable denim?
Some factories offer recycled fibres, water-saving washes or certified organic cotton. Claims need documents, so we ask for certificates and test reports before they appear on your labels.

### Can the factory handle private label packaging?
Yes. Labels, patches, hangtags and polybags can follow your artwork. We check them in the inspection. See [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).

### How are jeans shipped?
Jeans ship in cartons, usually folded or hung. Road freight serves Europe, while sea and air serve other markets. We quote options in 24h through our [logistics service](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I order denim fabric instead of finished jeans?
Yes. Some buyers purchase denim fabric from mills and make garments elsewhere. Define weight, composition, width and finish in the brief.

### How long does wash development take?
It depends on the effect. Simple washes can be fast, while complex finishes need several rounds. Your dated plan shows each stage.

### Can the factory do stretch and recycled denim?
Many factories offer stretch denim and some work with recycled fibres. Claims need documents, so we ask for certificates before they appear on labels.
