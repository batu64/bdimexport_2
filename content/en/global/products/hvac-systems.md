## Why Source Industrial HVAC from Turkey

HVAC equipment must perform as stated and arrive on the project schedule. Turkish manufacturers supply units and systems for buildings and industry, often with project engineering support and delivery in phases that follow the site programme.

- **Customs union with the EU.** Industrial goods made in Turkey can enter the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to rules of origin. Product rules such as CE marking still apply.

### Typical Order Terms

HVAC equipment is priced on capacity, components and options, so the offer should list the unit, accessories, controls and documentation separately. Projects often need delivery in phases that follow the site schedule, and we link shipments to those dates. Payment is usually split into milestones, such as a deposit at order, a payment at factory test and a balance at delivery. We help write test requirements, warranty terms, spare parts availability and commissioning scope into the contract. Installation is a separate scope unless the offer states that it is included.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Units ship crated or on pallets, by truck or container. Delivery is often split into phases that match the installation programme. We confirm dimensions, weights and handling requirements early, and check crating and labels before dispatch.

## Quality Control and Certification

HVAC equipment must perform as stated and meet the rules of your market.

- **Performance data:** capacity, efficiency and operating limits checked against the datasheet, with a test protocol where agreed.
- **Components:** compressors, heat exchangers, controls and electrical parts match the approved specification.
- **Conformity:** CE marking and declarations for the EU, and rules on refrigerants and energy efficiency that apply in your market. Confirm the rules for your destination.
- **Documentation and service:** manuals, wiring diagrams, spare parts and commissioning support in writing.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a measured, photographed report within 48 hours of the visit, and technical experts can join for complex equipment.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Unit type, capacity and components against the approved specification
- Nameplate data, refrigerant type and electrical supply
- Functional or performance test, with a protocol where agreed
- Safety devices, controls and wiring quality
- Documentation: manuals, wiring diagrams and conformity declarations
- Crating, protection and labelling by project area
