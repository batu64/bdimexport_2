## Why Source Steel from Turkey

Steel is bought on documents and tests, and a wrong grade is a safety risk. Turkish mills and fabricators supply long products and structures for projects abroad, and are used to certificates, third-party checks and loading supervision.

- **Local steel industry.** Turkey has a developed steel industry, which gives buyers several mills and fabricators to compare.
- **Standard and custom grades.** Producers supply common grades and sizes, and many offer cutting, bending and fabrication to your drawings.
- **Port access.** Ports on several coasts support bulk and breakbulk shipments of long products.
- **Near Europe and the Middle East.** Road and short sea routes serve project schedules in nearby regions.

## Quality Control and Certification

Steel is bought on documents and tests, and misidentified material is a safety risk.

- **Mill test certificates:** a certificate such as EN 10204 type 3.1 links the delivered material to a heat number with chemical and mechanical results.
- **Grade and dimensions:** grade markings, sizes, lengths, straightness and weight tolerance against the order and standard.
- **Third-party testing:** independent laboratory tests on samples when the project requires proof.
- **Conformity:** projects in the EU often require conformity documents for construction products and fabricated steel. Check what applies to your project.
- **Trade measures:** some markets apply import duties or trade defence measures on steel. Check them before you order.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) and loading supervision. The report follows within 48 hours of the visit.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Grade markings, sizes and lengths against the order and standard
- Heat numbers on bundles linked to mill test certificates
- Surface condition, straightness and visible defects
- Bundle count and weighing, compared with the packing list
- Samples drawn for independent laboratory tests, if required
- Lashing, protection and loading plan

| Risk | How we manage it |
| :--- | :--- |

### What is a mill test certificate?
It is a document from the producer that shows chemical and mechanical test results for a specific heat or batch of steel.

### Can I buy fabricated steel to my drawings?
Yes. Fabricators cut, bend, weld and coat steel according to drawings. We check dimensions, welds and finish during inspection.

### How do you verify quantity?
We check bundle counts and weights during loading supervision, compare them with the packing list and report any difference.

### Are there import duties on steel?
Some markets apply duties or trade measures. Check them with your customs adviser before you order. We help prepare the origin and product documents.

Yes. Large projects need rolling delivery dates, certificates per heat and tracking by lot, which we agree before the order.

### Can an independent surveyor check the lot?
Yes. We arrange inspection and sampling, and can involve a third-party surveyor if your contract or letter of credit requires one.
