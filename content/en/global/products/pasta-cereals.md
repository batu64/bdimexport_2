## Why Source Pasta and Cereals from Turkey

Dry food is simple to ship but sensitive to moisture, pests and labelling errors. Turkish producers pack pasta, pulses and bulgur for retail and food service, with experience in private label packing and the labels required by export markets.

- **Grain and pulse production at home.** Wheat, lentils, chickpeas and other crops are grown in the country, so producers have a local raw material base.
- **Many product formats.** Producers offer different pasta shapes, bulgur grades and cleaned, sorted pulses in bulk or consumer packs.
- **Private label packing.** Many packers fill for foreign brands and know label rules, barcodes and pallet requirements.
- **Reach to nearby markets.** Road and sea transport serve Europe, the Middle East and Africa, which supports regular replenishment.

## Working with pasta and cereal manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Define product and pack.** State the product, shape or grade, ingredients, cooking requirements, packaging size and label language. For pasta, define the durum wheat quality and the cooking time.
2. **Producer matching.** We shortlist producers and verify registration, food safety certificates, capacity and export history.
3. **Samples and tests.** We compare samples for appearance, cooking quality, foreign matter and moisture, and organise laboratory tests when your market requires them.
4. **Contract and production.** Specification, packing, labelling and delivery date are fixed in writing. Artwork is approved before printing.
5. **Inspection and shipping.** We check weight, packaging, labels, best-before dates and pallet condition, and supervise loading when needed.

### Typical Order Terms

Dry food prices depend on raw material costs, packaging, order size and the product format. Standard shapes and common pack sizes can be ordered in smaller quantities, while custom shapes, new moulds or special packaging need larger runs. Retail packs require approved artwork, correct label content and barcodes. We ask producers to quote on a clear basis such as EXW or FOB and to name what is included. Payment terms are agreed in writing, and best-before date requirements for your market are agreed in the specification so that goods arrive with enough shelf life.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Retail packs ship in cartons on pallets, and bulk goods in bags or big bags. Packing must protect against moisture and pests. We check pallets, wrapping and labels, and plan the container so that cartons are not crushed.

## Quality Control and Certification

Dry food is simple to store but sensitive to moisture, pests and labelling mistakes.

- **Product quality:** appearance, size, cooking behaviour, broken pieces and foreign matter against the specification.
- **Food safety:** certificates such as BRCGS, IFS, FSSC 22000 or ISO 22000, plus residue and contaminant tests required by your market.
- **Packaging and labelling:** ingredients, allergen information, best-before date, net weight and barcodes must be correct for the destination.
- **Storage and transport:** pallets and cartons should be dry, clean and protected from pests.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) and sampling for laboratory tests. The inspection report follows within 48 hours of the visit.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Product appearance, size, broken pieces and foreign matter
- Net weight and pack integrity on sampled units
- Label content, allergen information and best-before dates
- Barcodes and carton marking
- Cooking test on samples where specified
- Pallet quality, wrapping and container condition

### Common Risks and How We Manage Them

| Risk | How we manage it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cooking quality differs from the sample | Test cooking behaviour on samples and compare with the approved reference. |
| Foreign matter or insects | Specify cleaning standards, inspect goods and check storage conditions. |
| Label errors, allergen or date information | Approve label text in writing and check labels at inspection. |
| Moisture damage | Use moisture-protective packaging, dry pallets and suitable containers. |
| Short shelf life on arrival | Fix production date and best-before requirements in the contract. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I order pasta under my own brand?
Yes. Private label is common. You provide the design and label text, and the producer packs. We check artwork and finished packs against your approval.

### Which pulses can you source?
Common examples are lentils and chickpeas, and other pulses may be available. Tell us the variety and grade and we check availability.

### What does bulgur supply look like?
Bulgur is offered in different grain sizes and pack sizes for retail and food service. Define grain size and cooking requirement in your brief.

### What certificates should I ask for?
Food safety certificates such as BRCGS, IFS, FSSC 22000 or ISO 22000 are common, and organic certification where relevant. We verify them with the factory.

### What are the minimum quantities?
They depend on the product and the packing. Standard formats can ship in smaller quantities, while custom shapes or packaging need more.

### How is food packed for export?
Retail packs are placed in cartons on pallets, and bulk goods in bags or big bags. Packing must protect against moisture. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can you source gluten-free or specialty products?
Some producers make special products, but claims need documents and controls. Describe your requirement and we check what is possible.

### Do producers supply for food service?
Many do, with larger pack sizes. State pack sizes and cooking requirements for food service use.

### How do you check cooking quality?
We cook samples under defined conditions and compare texture, cooking loss and appearance with the approved sample.
