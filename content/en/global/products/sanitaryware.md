### Typical Order Terms

Sanitaryware is priced per piece or set, and prices depend on design, glaze, quantity and packing. Special colours and designs need larger runs. Heavy, fragile goods are expensive to ship, so container loading and packing quality affect the total cost. We ask manufacturers to state packing details in the offer, including carton type and inner protection. Payment terms are agreed in writing, normally with a deposit and a balance linked to inspection. Breakage handling is agreed in advance, for example a small allowance for damaged pieces and the way claims are reported. For project orders, we also agree phased delivery, the handling of spare pieces and carton labels by room or building, so that installers find the right items without opening every carton.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Breakage is the main cost risk. Individual cartons, inner protection, strong pallets and a loading plan reduce it. We check inner packing before loading, supervise loading for larger orders and arrange container or truck transport.

## Quality Control and Certification

Sanitaryware must work correctly and arrive unbroken.

- **Dimensions and installation fit:** size tolerance, hole positions and connection dimensions against the specification.
- **Surface and glaze:** cracks, pinholes, glaze defects, colour match and warping, graded against the agreed standard.
- **Function and standards:** flush performance, water consumption and strength according to recognised European standards where relevant to your market. Ask the producer for test reports.
- **Packing:** individual cartons with inner protection, strong pallets and good loading, because breakage is the main cost risk.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a photographed, measured report within 48 hours of the visit, and loading supervision when requested.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Dimensions, hole positions and connection fit against drawings
- Glaze quality, cracks, warping and colour match
- Function: flush, drainage and fixing points where applicable
- Set contents: accessories, fittings and fixings in each carton
- Carton strength, inner protection and labelling
- Pallet build and loading plan

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you reduce breakage?
By checking inner packing, carton strength and pallet stability before loading, and by supervising loading for large orders.

### Can I buy a matching bathroom set?
Often yes. Many manufacturers offer series with matching pieces, and some also supply fittings. Mixed orders may need several suppliers.

### What tests are important?
Dimensions, flush function, water consumption and strength. Ask for test reports according to the standards your market uses.

### Is private label possible?
Some producers accept private label or custom branding. Tooling and minimums depend on the design. See [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).

### What minimum order is typical?
It depends on the product and the colour. Standard white products can ship in smaller quantities, while special colours or designs need larger runs.

### How is sanitaryware shipped?
Palletised in containers or by truck, with load planning to reduce damage. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I order complete bathroom sets?
Many manufacturers offer matching series. Fittings and furniture may come from other suppliers, and we can coordinate them.

### What colours are available?
White is most common. Other colours need larger runs and sampling. Show a colour reference to the manufacturer.

### What happens if some pieces break in transit?
Report damage with photographs on arrival. Claims depend on the contract and insurance, so agree the process before shipping.
