## Why Source Agricultural Machinery from Turkey

Farm machinery must work season after season in tough conditions, so buyers look at build quality, service and approvals. Turkish manufacturers supply tractors and implements for many crops, and offer options that can be adapted to your market.

- **Experience with varied farming.** Turkish agriculture ranges from small orchards to large grain farms, so manufacturers offer machines for different conditions.
- **Tractors and implements together.** Many suppliers offer tractors and matching implements, which simplifies service and spare parts planning.
- **Adaptation to local conditions.** Builders adapt machines to soil, crop and power requirements, and offer options that suit your market.
- **Customs union with the EU.** Industrial goods made in Turkey can enter the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to rules of origin. Vehicle and machinery approval rules still apply.

### Typical Order Terms

Farm machinery is priced on power, configuration and options, so the offer should list the base machine, attachments and accessories as separate items. Orders are often seasonal, so we plan production and shipping around your selling or working season. Payment is usually split into milestones, such as a deposit and a balance against inspection or documents. We help write warranty terms, parts availability, operator training and documentation into the contract. Approvals required in your country are confirmed in writing before the order, because a machine without the right approval cannot be sold or registered.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Tractors and large implements ship on trucks, in containers or on roll-on roll-off vessels, depending on size. Lashing and protection are part of the plan. We confirm dimensions and weights early and check documents before dispatch.

## Quality Control and Certification

Farm machinery works in dust, mud and heat, so build quality and service support matter as much as price.

- **Specification and build:** engine and transmission data, welds, paint, hydraulics, safety guards and attachments against the order.
- **Approval and conformity:** many markets require type approval or conformity marking for tractors and machines. Check your destination rules before ordering.
- **Documentation:** operator manuals in your language, parts catalogue and warranty terms.
- **Service planning:** spare parts list, wear parts stock and training for your dealer or operators.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a photographed, measured report within 48 hours of the visit. Technical experts can join for complex machines.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Machine configuration against the signed specification
- Engine, transmission and hydraulic data on the nameplate
- Function test of key systems: lifting, PTO, hydraulics and lights
- Welds, paint, guards and safety decals
- Manuals, parts catalogue and conformity documents
- Lashing, protection and loading plan
