## Why Source Furniture from Turkey

Furniture is bought on appearance, comfort and strength, and returns are expensive when assembly fails. Turkish factories supply many styles and price levels, offer flat-pack and assembled options, and ship efficiently to European markets.

- **A large furniture manufacturing base.** Many factories produce upholstered, wooden and panel furniture, with different styles and price levels.
- **Design and customisation.** Manufacturers offer catalogue ranges and can adapt dimensions, finishes and fabrics for your market.
- **Flat-pack and assembled options.** You can choose flat-pack for transport efficiency or assembled pieces for sofas and upholstered items.
- **Short routes to Europe.** Road transport serves many European destinations, which suits furniture volumes and delivery windows.

## Working with furniture manufacturers in Turkey: Step by Step

1. **Define the range.** State product types, materials, dimensions, finishes, fabrics, flat-pack or assembled, packaging and quantities. Photos and reference products help.
2. **Factory matching and verification.** We shortlist factories that already make similar pieces and verify registration, capacity and references.
3. **Samples and approval.** We compare samples with your specification: dimensions, materials, finish, hardware, assembly and comfort. We record every change and approve a reference sample.
4. **Production and checks.** We follow the schedule and check materials and the first finished pieces. For large orders we plan a mid-production check.
5. **Inspection and loading.** We inspect finished goods, test assembly on samples from the lot, check packing and supervise loading to maximise container use and limit damage.

### What to Prepare Before You Request a Quotation

- Product list with photos, drawings or reference products
- Materials: board, wood, metal, fabric or leather, and thicknesses
- Finishes and colours, with samples where possible
- Hardware and mechanisms: hinges, runners, sofa beds

- Safety, flammability and emission requirements of your market

### Typical Order Terms

Furniture prices depend on materials, finish, hardware, packing and quantity. Flat-pack products save freight volume, while assembled upholstery costs more to ship. We ask factories to quote materials, finish, hardware and packing in clear items, and to confirm lead time by model. Colour and fabric changes require samples and approval. Payment terms are agreed in writing, normally with a deposit and a balance linked to inspection. For e-commerce, packing strength and instruction quality decide returns, so they are part of the approval of the first sample. We also agree how cartons are labelled, whether spare fittings are included and how missing or damaged items are reported, so that after-sales claims can be handled quickly and fairly.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Furniture uses a lot of volume, so a load plan saves freight and reduces damage. Flat-pack cartons are stacked and secured, and assembled upholstery needs more space. We check packing, supervise loading when requested and arrange container or truck transport.

## Quality Control and Certification

Furniture quality includes appearance, strength and safety, and each can fail in a different way.

- **Materials and finish:** board type and thickness, wood or veneer, fabric, foam density, paint or lacquer, and colour match to the approved sample.
- **Construction and function:** joints, hardware, drawers, doors, stability and assembly, including a trial assembly of flat-pack items.
- **Safety and standards:** relevant strength, stability and flammability requirements for your market, and emission limits for wood-based panels. Tell us which apply.
- **Packing:** carton strength, corner protection and labels with product and assembly information.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a photographed, measured report within 48 hours of the visit. For private label ranges, see [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).

### What We Check at Inspection

- Dimensions, finish and colour against the approved sample
- Joints, stability, drawers, doors and hardware function
- Trial assembly of flat-pack items from the lot
- Parts count, fittings and assembly instructions
- Carton strength, corner protection and labels
- Load plan to use the container well and limit damage

### Common Risks and How We Manage Them

| Risk | How we manage it |
| :--- | :--- |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I choose flat-pack or assembled?
Flat-pack saves freight volume and reduces damage, but needs assembly. Upholstered pieces are usually shipped assembled. We help compare total cost.

### Can I change materials or colours?
Often yes. Fabrics, finishes and dimensions can be adapted. New options require samples and approval.

### How do you check flat-pack quality?
We assemble sample items from the lot, check hole alignment, parts count, instructions and stability, and record findings with photographs.

### What is the minimum order?
It depends on the product and the factory. Catalogue items can ship in smaller quantities, while custom designs need larger runs.

### How do I avoid damage in transit?
Strong cartons, corner protection, careful loading and a good load plan reduce damage. We check packing and can supervise loading.

### How is furniture shipped?
By container or truck. Load planning is important because furniture uses much volume. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I mix several models in one container?
Yes. Mixed loading is common, and a load plan keeps heavy items low and protects fragile ones.

### Can I add my own brand and packaging?
Yes. Many factories offer private label. Artwork and labels are approved in writing. See [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).

### How do I avoid returns from e-commerce customers?
Approve clear instructions, test assembly, check packing strength and use quality checks on finish and hardware.
