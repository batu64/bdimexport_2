### Typical Order Terms

Kitchenware prices depend on material, thickness, finish, packaging and quantity. Sets with gift boxes and printed packaging need approved artwork and larger runs. We ask manufacturers to quote product, packaging and any private label items as clear lines. Payment terms are agreed in writing, normally with a deposit and a balance linked to inspection. Seasonal peaks in the retail calendar affect lead time, so we confirm production plans before the order. Food contact documents are requested together with the offer, because retailers often need them before listing a product. For gift sets, we agree the packaging design, barcode and carton marking at the start, because late changes to printed packaging cause delay and extra cost.

### Packing and Shipping Notes

Kitchenware is often heavy and breakable. Inner dividers, strong cartons and good pallets reduce breakage. We check packaging and labels, supervise loading when asked and arrange container or truck transport, with care for mixed loads.

## Quality Control and Certification

Kitchenware touches food and heat, so safety and durability come first.

- **Food contact compliance:** materials must meet the food contact rules of your market, for example the EU framework regulation, and test reports should cover the product you buy.
- **Material and construction:** stainless steel grade, thickness, welds, handle strength, coating quality and lid fit.
- **Performance:** heat distribution, induction compatibility, dishwasher resistance and scratch resistance, depending on your claims.
- **Presentation and packing:** surface finish, printing, gift boxes, barcodes and cartons that protect glass and ceramic.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) with a photographed, measured report within 48 hours of the visit. For brand launches see [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).

### What We Check at Inspection

- Dimensions, weight and thickness against the approved sample
- Surface, coating, edges, handles and lid fit
- Function checks such as stability, pouring and heating behaviour
- Food contact documents and markings against the order
- Gift boxes, labels, barcodes and printed packaging
- Carton strength and inner protection for glass and ceramic

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I order cookware under my own brand?
Yes. Many manufacturers offer private label. You provide logos, packaging design and labels, and we check the finished products against your approval.

### What food contact documents are needed?
Test reports and declarations of compliance for the materials, according to the rules of your market. Ask for reports on the actual product.

### How do you check stainless steel quality?
We ask for material grade documents, check thickness and finish and may test samples in a laboratory. Magnet tests are not conclusive.

### What is the minimum order?
It depends on the product, finish and packaging. Standard items can ship in smaller quantities, while custom designs or packaging need larger runs.

### How do you reduce breakage for ceramic and glass?
With strong inner packing, correct carton size and careful loading. We check packing during inspection.

### How is kitchenware shipped?
Palletised in containers or by truck. Weight and fragility drive packing and loading. See [freight logistics](/en/services/logistics).

### Can I order complete cookware sets?
Yes. Many manufacturers offer sets with matching pieces and gift packaging. Define set contents and packaging in the brief.

### Is induction compatibility tested?
It can be tested on samples. State the requirement in the specification so that the manufacturer builds and tests accordingly.

### Do you help launch a private label kitchenware range?
Yes. We shortlist manufacturers, manage samples and inspect goods. See [private label manufacturing](/en/services/manufacturing).
