## Quality Control and Certification

Hazelnuts are checked for size, freshness and, above all, safety.

- **Grading:** calibre, kernel colour, defects, shell fragments and foreign matter.
- **Moisture and freshness:** moisture level and smell. Rancid or mouldy nuts must be rejected.
- **Mycotoxins:** aflatoxin is the main safety issue for hazelnuts. Many markets enforce strict limits and checks, so we recommend testing each lot before shipment.
- **Food safety systems:** look for BRCGS, IFS, FSSC 22000 or ISO 22000, and organic certification when needed.

We arrange [factory inspection](/en/services/inspection) and sampling for laboratory tests. The inspection report follows within 48 hours of the visit, and laboratory costs are listed separately.

### What We Check at Inspection

- Kernel calibre, colour and defects on drawn samples
- Moisture, rancidity smell and signs of mould
- Samples drawn for aflatoxin and other laboratory tests
- Sacks, bags or cartons: condition, weight and marking
- Batch numbers, crop year and traceability records
- Pallet stability and container cleanliness before loading

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What forms of hazelnuts can I buy?
In shell, natural kernels, blanched kernels, roasted nuts, sliced, chopped, ground and paste are common. Tell us your use and we match the product.

### How are hazelnuts graded?
By calibre (kernel size), colour, defect level and moisture. The specification should name each parameter and the tolerance you accept.

### Why is aflatoxin testing important?
Aflatoxin is a natural contaminant that can occur in nuts. Markets such as the EU enforce strict limits, so testing each lot protects you from rejected shipments.

### Can I buy organic hazelnuts?
Some processors offer organic hazelnuts. We ask for the current certificate and check that it covers the lot you buy.

### What packaging is used?
Vacuum or sealed bags in cartons, or sacks for in-shell nuts, are common. Retail packs and private label packing are also possible.

### When can I order?
The harvest is in late summer and early autumn, and processors supply throughout the year from stock. Crop year and stock level influence price and availability.

### What is the difference between natural and blanched kernels?
Natural kernels keep their skin, while blanched kernels have the skin removed. The choice depends on your recipe and appearance requirements.

### Can you source hazelnut paste or ingredients?
Some processors make hazelnut paste and ground products for confectionery. Describe your recipe and specification in the brief.

### How are in-shell hazelnuts packed?
Usually in sacks or big bags. Packing must keep the nuts dry and allow ventilation as needed.
