# Textile & Apparel Manufacturers in Turkey | B2B Sourcing

Turkish textile production covers the chain from yarn and fabric to finished garments and home textiles. For a buyer, this means a complete collection can often be sourced in one country, with shorter distances to European and regional markets than many other origins.

## What buyers usually source

Apparel and ready-to-wear, home textiles and bedding, denim, and technical or nonwoven fabrics. Each has its own quality questions, so each has its own product page.

## What to specify before you ask for prices

- Fibre composition, weight and finishing, with a reference sample where possible
- Minimum order per colour and size, and the delivery window you need
- Labelling, packaging and any standards your market or retailer requires, such as an OEKO-TEX certificate
- The Incoterm you want to compare offers on
