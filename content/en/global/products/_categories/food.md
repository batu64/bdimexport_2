# Food & Agriculture Manufacturers in Turkey
Turkey grows and processes a wide range of food products, from olive oil and nuts to dried fruit, pulses and pasta. Buyers can choose bulk supply for further processing or retail-ready packs under their own brand.

## What buyers usually source

Extra virgin olive oil, dried fruits such as figs, apricots and raisins, hazelnuts and nut ingredients, and pasta, pulses and cereals. Each product page covers its own specification and checks.

## What to specify before you ask for prices

- Product grade, size or variety, and any quality parameters you need
- Packaging type, label language and shelf-life requirements
- Food safety certificates you expect, for example BRCGS or IFS, and the analyses required by your market
- Quantity per order, harvest or delivery timing and the Incoterm
