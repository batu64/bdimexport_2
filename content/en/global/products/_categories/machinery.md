# Industrial Machinery Manufacturers in Turkey | B2B Sourcing

Turkish machine builders supply equipment for manufacturing, buildings, water and agriculture. Machinery purchases are technical, so the main risk is a mismatch between what you need and what the supplier can build, test and support.

## What buyers usually source

CNC machine tools and lasers, industrial HVAC and chillers, pumps, valves and fluid engineering equipment, and agricultural machinery and tractors.

## What to specify before you ask for prices

- Technical specification, capacity and operating conditions, with drawings where they exist
- Applicable standards and the conformity marking your market requires, such as CE marking
- Commissioning, training, spare parts and after-sales support expectations
- Acceptance tests you want to witness before shipment
