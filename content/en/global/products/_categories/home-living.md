# Home & Living Manufacturers in Turkey
Home and living covers products people use every day, so appearance, finish and packaging decide whether customers keep them. Turkish manufacturers supply furniture, kitchenware and carpets for retail, e-commerce and contract projects.

## What buyers usually source

Modular living room and bedroom furniture, contract furniture for hospitality, kitchenware and tabletop decor, and carpets and rugs. Home textiles and bedding are listed in the textile category.

## What to specify before you ask for prices

- Materials, finish and colour, with a reference sample
- Whether the product ships assembled or flat-packed, and who assembles it
- Packaging that protects the product in transport, and the labels your market needs
- Volume per model, delivery window and the Incoterm
