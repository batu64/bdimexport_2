### Calculation Notes

The total adds the product groups that carry no "of which" label. Each change is calculated as the 2025 value minus the 2023 value, divided by the 2023 value. Rows marked "of which" are shown for information. Their codes are already part of another row, so they are not added again.

### What the Codes Include

- **Natural Stone, Marble & Travertine.** Heading 2515 covers marble, travertine and similar stone in blocks or rough slabs. Heading 6802 covers worked stone, such as tiles, slabs and articles, in marble and in other stones. The total includes some non-marble worked stone.
- **Ceramic & Porcelain Stoneware.** Heading 6907 covers ceramic tiles, including porcelain stoneware, for walls and floors. The tile headings were restructured in the 2022 revision of the Harmonized System, so we show only years that use the current structure.
- **Structural Steel & Rebar.** Headings 7213, 7214 and 7216 cover hot-rolled bars, rebar, wire rod and sections, and heading 7308 covers steel structures and parts. Flat steel products such as sheet and plate are reported under other headings, so the total covers long products and structures only.
- **Sanitaryware & Architectural Fittings.** Heading 6910 covers ceramic sinks, baths, toilet bowls and similar fixtures, and heading 7324 covers sanitary ware of iron or steel, including stainless steel. Taps and valves are reported under heading 8481, and plastic or acrylic products under other headings.

## Trends Shaping Construction Materials Sourcing

### Natural stone and tiles: shade and size
Exports of marble and worked stone in 2025 were higher than in 2023 and exports of ceramic tiles were about the same as in 2023. For both, the risk is variation between lots. Approve a reference range, reserve the whole quantity from one lot or production run and inspect shade, size and flatness before loading.

### Steel: documents and trade measures
Exports of the steel long products and structures in the table were higher than in 2023. Steel is bought on documents, so ask for mill test certificates linked to heat numbers. Check import duties and trade measures in your market before you order.

### Sanitaryware: breakage drives cost
Exports of sanitaryware were lower than in 2023. The main cost risk is breakage in transit, so check inner packing, carton strength and the loading plan, and agree how damaged pieces are reported.

## Turkey's Position for Buyers

- **Raw materials and processing in one country.** Quarries, tile plants, steel mills and ceramic factories operate in the same country, which gives you several suppliers to compare for each product family.
- **Ports for heavy cargo.** Ports on the Aegean, Mediterranean and other coasts support container, bulk and breakbulk shipments, and road links serve nearby markets.
- **Customs union with the EU.** Industrial goods made in Turkey can enter the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to rules of origin and product rules. Please check the details for your product.
- **Project supply.** Many producers supply both catalogue products and project orders, with matching trims, accessories and documentation.
