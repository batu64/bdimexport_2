### Calculation Notes

The total adds the product groups that carry no "of which" label. Each change is calculated as the 2025 value minus the 2023 value, divided by the 2023 value. Rows marked "of which" are shown for information. Their codes are already part of another row, so they are not added again.

### What the Codes Include

- **Modular Living & Bedroom Furniture.** Heading 9401 covers seats and 9403 covers other furniture, for example for bedrooms, offices and kitchens. Heading 9401 also includes seats for vehicles, so the total includes some non-domestic seating.
- **Kitchenware & Tabletop Decor.** Heading 7323 covers household articles of iron and steel, headings 6911 and 6912 cover porcelain and other ceramic tableware, and heading 7615 covers household articles of aluminium. Glassware, cutlery and plastic products are reported elsewhere.
- **Carpets & Rugs.** Heading 5701 covers knotted carpets, 5702 covers woven carpets and 5703 covers tufted carpets. Felt carpets and other floor coverings are reported under other headings, so the total covers these three types.
- **Contract Hospitality Furniture.** These four codes cover wooden-frame seats and wooden bedroom and other wooden furniture for any use. Trade statistics do not report hotel or contract furniture separately, so the figure is a broad indicator for wooden furniture.

## Trends Shaping Home & Living Sourcing

### Furniture: flat-pack or assembled
Furniture exports in 2025 were about the same as in 2023. Flat-pack saves freight volume and damage, while assembled upholstery costs more to ship. Test assembly on samples from the lot and check hardware, instructions and carton strength.

### Contract furniture: approve a room sample
The wooden furniture codes used as a proxy for hospitality furniture were about the same as in 2023. These codes do not separate contract furniture, so rely on a signed room sample and a phased delivery plan linked to the opening date of the project.

### Kitchenware and carpets: documents and presentation
Kitchenware exports were lower than in 2023 and carpet exports were about the same as in 2023. For kitchenware, ask for food contact test reports on the actual product. For carpets, approve a physical sample for colour and feel, because screens do not show colour accurately.

## Turkey's Position for Buyers

- **A broad manufacturing base.** Many factories produce furniture, kitchenware and carpets at different price levels and quality grades, so you can match the supplier to your market.
- **Design and private label.** Manufacturers offer catalogue ranges and also build to your design or under your brand. Artwork and packaging stay under your control.
- **Short routes to Europe.** Road transport serves many European destinations, which suits bulky products and regular replenishment.
- **Project capability.** Contract manufacturers work to designer drawings and build mock-ups for approval, then deliver in phases that follow the building programme.
