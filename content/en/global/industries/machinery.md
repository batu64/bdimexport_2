### Calculation Notes

The total adds the product groups that carry no "of which" label. Each change is calculated as the 2025 value minus the 2023 value, divided by the 2023 value. Rows marked "of which" are shown for information. Their codes are already part of another row, so they are not added again.

### What the Codes Include

- **CNC Machine Tools & Lasers.** Headings 8456 to 8459 and 8462 cover machine tools for metal working, including laser machines, machining centres, lathes, drilling and milling machines and sheet metal forming machines. The codes do not separate CNC machines from conventional ones, and they exclude tooling and software.
- **Industrial HVAC & Chillers.** These codes cover central heating boilers (8403), air conditioning machines (8415) and refrigerating equipment and heat pumps (8418). They include household appliances such as refrigerators and room air conditioners, so the figure overstates industrial HVAC and should be read as a broad indicator.
- **Pumps, Valves & Fluid Engineering.** Heading 8413 covers pumps for liquids and 8481 covers taps, cocks and valves. Both headings include household and vehicle uses next to industrial equipment, so the total is a broad indicator of the fluid handling sector.
- **Agricultural Machinery & Tractors.** Heading 8701 covers all tractors, including road tractors, not only agricultural tractors. Headings 8432, 8433 and 8436 cover soil preparation, harvesting and other agricultural machinery. The total is therefore broader than farm equipment alone.

## Trends Shaping Machinery & Engineering Sourcing

### Machine tools: compare on the same basis
Exports of metal-working machine tools in 2025 were lower than in 2023. Do not use totals to judge a builder. Compare offers on the same specification, accuracy, control system, accessories, delivery time and support, and agree an acceptance test on your own part.

### HVAC, pumps and valves: read the scope notes
Exports of the HVAC codes were about the same as in 2023 and exports of the pump and valve codes were higher than in 2023. Both groups include household and vehicle items, so they overstate industrial equipment. For a project, ask for datasheets, material certificates and a witnessed test.

### Agricultural machinery: approvals and service
Exports of the agricultural machinery codes were lower than in 2023. The tractor code includes all tractors. Before you order, confirm the approvals your country requires and the spare parts and service you will receive.

## Turkey's Position for Buyers

- **An industrial base.** Automotive, white goods, metalworking and construction industries create domestic demand, and builders are used to customer specifications and repeat orders.
- **Customisation and support.** Many builders adapt machines to your parts and offer training and commissioning. Local engineering teams can answer technical questions quickly.
- **Customs union with the EU.** Industrial goods made in Turkey can enter the European Union under the EU-Turkey customs union, subject to rules of origin. Product rules such as CE marking still apply and must be checked.
- **Practical logistics.** Heavy equipment ships by truck or container, with short transit to nearby regions. Lifting points, lashing and protection are part of the packing plan.
